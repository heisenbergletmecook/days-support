DAYS for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

DAYS is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or a DAYS server. The information you enter is stored by the app on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

When you create a countdown, DAYS stores the information needed to provide it: a unique identifier, title, date, time-zone reference, category, repeat setting, reminder choice, optional notes, and created and updated dates. DAYS also stores app preferences such as whether reminders and app lock are enabled.

This information is stored in the app’s local data container on your iPhone. DAYS does not provide an account, cloud sync, export, or a backend service.

Data collection and tracking

The developer of DAYS does not collect the content of your countdowns or app preferences. The released app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, third-party tracking SDK, or network client used to send this information to the developer or another company.

DAYS does not track you across apps or websites and does not sell personal information.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, DAYS asks iOS for notification permission. The app schedules local notifications on your device. A pending notification contains the countdown title and a short reminder message. It is delivered by iOS without a DAYS server.

Your notification preview may be visible on the Lock Screen depending on your iPhone notification settings. You can change permission or preview behavior in iOS Settings.

Face ID, Touch ID, and device passcode

If you enable app lock, DAYS asks Apple’s Local Authentication system to verify you with the authentication method available on your device. Biometric matching is handled by iOS. DAYS receives only whether authentication succeeded; it does not receive or store your face, fingerprint, optic, or passcode data.

Backups

DAYS does not perform cloud sync. However, its local app data may be included in an iPhone backup managed by Apple, depending on your device and backup settings. Apple’s handling of backups is governed by Apple’s policies and your Apple account settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete one countdown from its edit screen, or use Settings → Privacy → Erase All Data to remove all countdowns and cancel their pending reminders. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data. Copies may remain in device backups until those backups are replaced or deleted through Apple’s backup controls.

Apple services

DAYS uses Apple frameworks for local storage, notifications, and device authentication. Apple may separately process App Store, diagnostic, or backup information under Apple’s own policies and your device settings. DAYS does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting service.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how DAYS handles data, this policy will be updated before those practices are represented here. The effective date at the top shows when this policy was last revised.
